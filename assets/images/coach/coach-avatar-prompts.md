# Coach Avatar Prompt Pack

These prompts map to the real coach personas currently implemented in the app.

Premium access reality:
- `Gentle Supportive` / `Doux Bienveillant` is free.
- `Strict Tough`, `Motivational Energetic`, `Patient Calm`, `Analytical Precise`, and `Playful Light` are premium personas.

Shared series rules for every generation:
- Bust or portrait framing only.
- Premium stylized human portrait, semi-illustration / semi-soft-3D.
- Dark restrained background with a soft aura and subtle futuristic light geometry.
- Clean backdrop, no clutter, no scene storytelling, no props unless minimal and integrated.
- Designed for a small app avatar: strong facial readability, clean silhouette, clear expression.
- Warm but sober, elegant, slightly futuristic, never kitsch.
- No text, no logo, no watermark, no UI chrome.
- Keep the whole series visually consistent in lighting, lens feel, polish, and composition.

## Gentle Supportive

Free persona.

Visual intention:
- Warm, reassuring, approachable, soft emotional safety.

Main prompt:
```text
Premium stylized portrait avatar of a gentle supportive wellness coach, bust framing, calm reassuring expression, kind eyes, soft posture, rounded silhouette, elegant semi-illustration mixed with soft 3D rendering, dark navy background with a subtle blue and amber aura, minimal futuristic halo geometry behind the head, clean composition, refined cinematic rim light, highly readable at small size, premium wellness app aesthetic, warm but understated, no clutter, no text, no logo, no watermark
```

Short prompt:
```text
Gentle supportive coach avatar, premium stylized bust portrait, warm reassuring face, dark navy background, soft blue-amber aura, clean futuristic wellness app style
```

Negative prompt:
```text
cartoon, childish, exaggerated smile, hyper realistic skin pores, harsh realism, busy background, props, text, logo, watermark, neon overload, fantasy costume, comedy style, horror lighting, distorted anatomy
```

Framing notes:
- Portrait avatar, head and upper chest visible.
- Keep the face centered and open.
- Background should stay dark and minimal with a soft blue-amber glow.
- Prioritize softness and trust over drama.

Series consistency:
- Use the same lens feel and halo system as the other coaches.
